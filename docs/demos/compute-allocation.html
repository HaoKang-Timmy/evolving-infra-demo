Animation over three hours of serving on nine nodes. The workload shifts from math agents (Putnam) to a mix and then to computer-use agents (OSWorld), and the optimal split rises from 3 to 4 prefill nodes, to 5 to 6, and then to 7 to 8. An existing planner (Dynamo) keeps fewer prefill nodes than the optimal split for most of the run, and for most of the run one of its nodes is restarting. Evolving Infra moves decode nodes to prefill and reaches the optimal split in each phase, from 3P6D to 6P3D and then 8P1D.
