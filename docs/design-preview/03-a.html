The lines are the PD split of each system in the report’s shifting-agent-mix run: Kimi K2.6 on nine nodes of 4× GB300, with 288 Putnam agents, then 144 Putnam and 72 OSWorld agents, then 144 OSWorld agents, one hour each.

The best split per 10 min and the best static split are profiled under SGLang PD disaggregation. In the OSWorld phase, Evolving Infra settles at 8P1D, one prefill node more, because its symmetric engines shift the best split.
