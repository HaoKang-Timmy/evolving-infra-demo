Design 03-F — Moving wallWork from the agents falls on prefill or decode nodes: behind a fixed wall one side piles up while the other sits idle, and Evolving Infra slides the wall so both sides stay busy.

Move the wall. Keep both busy.

Animation: two clusters of nine GPU nodes. A wall separates prefill nodes from decode nodes, and work from the agents falls on each side. The agents change from math agents to computer-use agents, so more work falls on the prefill side. With the fixed split, work piles up on the prefill side while several decode nodes sit idle. Evolving Infra slides its wall and turns three decode nodes into prefill nodes, so nothing piles up and both sides stay busy.
